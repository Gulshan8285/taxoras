1. **Affordable USP Hook**: Every article must explicitly cite the starting price (e.g. GST retainers from ₹799/month, TDS from ₹999/quarter) within the first 150 words.
2. **Zero Fabricated Claims**: No invented certifications, imaginary review counts, or unverified CA partnerships. Cite direct sections of CGST Act 2017 and Income Tax Act 1961.
3. **Direct WhatsApp Conversion Block**: Every article must include an amber-accented WhatsApp CTA card with a tailored pre-filled message parameter (`https://wa.me/918810381949?text=...`).
4. **GEO / AEO Answer Card**: Place a concise 45-word direct answer block in an alert box right after the H1 for Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT Search crawlers.
