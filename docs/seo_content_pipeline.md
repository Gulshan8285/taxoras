# High-Impact SEO & GEO Content Pipeline
## India Accounting, GST & Taxation Advisory (2026 Strategy)

**Strategic Objective:** Establish topical authority in search engines (Google, Bing) and AI search engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) through evidence-based, technically rigorous compliance content without spam or thin doorway pages.

## The 10 Prioritized Pillar & Cluster Articles

### 1. GST Return Filing Charges in India: A Pricing Comparison
- **Primary Keyword:** `gst return filing charges in india`
- **Secondary Keywords:** `gst filing fees per month`, `gstr 3b filing cost`, `online gst return filing price`
- **Search Intent:** Commercial Investigation / Pricing transparency.
- **Target Audience:** Traders, MSME manufacturers, and e-commerce sellers needing to budget monthly compliance expenses.
- **Core Content Gap:** Competitors quote "₹499/mo" but hide invoice limits (e.g. only up to 10 invoices). The article provides an invoice-volume matrix (<50, 50-200, 200-500) and explains additional costs like GSTR-9 annual return and GSTR-2B ITC matching.
- **Internal Service Links:** `/services/gst-return-filing`, `/pricing`.
- **Authoritative Citations:** CBIC Notification on GSTR-3B late fees, Section 47 of CGST Act.
